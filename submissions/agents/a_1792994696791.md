### Beskrivelse

```text
CLI wrapper around @companydata/companydata-sdk for Danish CVR company data (addresses, phone, email, website, firmographics) via one REST call. Depends on a third-party commercial SDK layer rather than Erhvervsstyrelsen's own free CVR/Virk API directly.
```
